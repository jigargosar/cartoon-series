# 001: Cartoon Animation Viewer

## What we're building

A cartoon animation viewer with a sketchy hand-drawn look.

## What it shows

- Scene 1 — A green ball slides across the screen with overshoot
- Scene 2 — A red box drops from above and squashes on landing

## What the user can do

- Play, pause, scrub through the animation

## Style

- Sketchy hand-drawn look
